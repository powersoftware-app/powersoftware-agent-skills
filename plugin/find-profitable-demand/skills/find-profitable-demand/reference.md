## §B — Validation arithmetic (the hard gate)

### B.1 KGR (Keyword Gold Ratio) — quantify a blue-ocean / new word

```
KGR = ( number of results for  allintitle:"<exact phrase>" )  /  ( monthly search volume )
```

- Take the `allintitle:` count (pages with the phrase in their `<title>`) as **真实竞争供应量**.
- **KGR < 1** and the allintitle result **≤ ~3 pages** ⇒ a genuinely winnable low-competition word.
- 哥飞's point: "刚出现的词也不一定竞争少" — the community floods new words fast, so *measure*, don't guess.
- Old words: don't trust the KD number alone — check the **real page supply** (高 KD 也能做，先看真实网页供应量).

### B.2 共识 (consensus) — why invented names rank for nothing

《【哥飞SEO理论】搜索量来自于共识》+《谷歌排名的真相》: search volume exists only for words people
*already* agree on. A brand-new coined brand is searched by ~no one → 0 traffic → ranks for nothing.
**Pick the market word first, name the brand second.** (This is also why the home `<title>` must carry a
positioning keyword, not just the brand — see plan-seo-site.)

### B.3 第一美元算术 (first-dollar ROI)

《哥飞教你如何赚到你的第一美元？》:
- Assume ~1% conversion → 1 order/day needs ~100 clicks/day from Google.
- At a ~5% CTR (not #1) → that needs a word with ~2000 impressions/day.
- Score candidates by **ROI = (volume × CPC) / KD**: `>200` 强烈推荐 · `50–200` 可做 · `<50` 跳过.
- **Beginner bars** (first site = learn the loop, not max revenue): `KD < 10`, referring domains `< 10`,
  and even **~100/mo volume** is fine just to close the loop once.

### B.4 Candidate-verdict table (format to reproduce)

| keyword | us vol | KD | real allintitle supply | who monetizes it | verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| `<task word>` | … | … | small / medium / red-ocean | ads? subs? AdSense-rich niche? | do / watch / skip |

Read every row as *volume (共识)* × *real supply (not just KD)* × *proof it pays*.

## §D — Launch recipes & stack (Phase 3 & 5 details)

### D.1 Two end-to-end demo recipes

- **半小时上线一个小游戏站** (memorytest.io example): make page → **Vercel** deploy → add the **2 DNS
  records Vercel gives** into **Cloudflare** → point domain → submit to **GSC**. Game/new-word sites
  give the fastest feedback loop for a first 练手站.
- **10分钟上线导航 + 博客站** (GitBase): star `github.com/qiayue/GitBase`, use GitHub-as-CMS; also
  《新人友好、完全无需数据却有后台、可动态更新内容的开源 CMS》— static front-end (100/100 performance)
  + a content-managing back end.

### D.2 Supporting tooling articles

- 《推荐七个可以直接复制代码拿来就用的前端代码网站》 — copy-paste front-end to ship fast.
- 《如何利用各种工具制作一个 SVG 格式的 Logo》 + favicon tooling — cheap brand assets (don't over-invest).
- 《哥飞的快速上站 SOP（手动版）》 — the manual checklist the above recipes come from.

### D.3 Monetization notes (Phase 5)

- **AdSense 提交申请的小细节** (出海教程) — enough real content, clear nav + policy pages, don't pile
  duplicate/thin pages before review. A member's new site passed review on **day 13**.
- 《我如何通过 SEO 和 Adsense 优化，把一个网站广告收入从每月八百多美元提升到每月两千多美元》 —
  same traffic, ~2.5× revenue from placement + traffic-quality tuning.
- 《大白话讲清楚出海网站赚钱逻辑：为什么能赚钱？赚谁的钱？怎么赚？需要做什么？》 — the mental model of
  AdSense arbitrage (Google pays you for serving intent-matched ads to search visitors).
- Paid/subscription/affiliate paths and the multi-site matrix; to actually sell a license-enabled
  product use the sibling skills **publish-product** + **integrate-license**.

### D.4 Promo channels (Phase 4, non-SEO)

《哥飞：AI出海产品如何搞流量？》 lists seven: SEO · SEM · 投流 · 邮件营销 · 发帖宣传(X/即刻) · 软文营销 ·
红人/达人营销. Extras seen in the corpus:
- **X 涨粉**：不断发"让人想收藏"的优质内容 → 转发裂变 (哥飞 @gefei55).
- **Reddit**：3天从负数到 300+ karma 再发；既验证需求又导首批流量.
- **AI-SEO / GEO**：《以 DeepSeek 为例聊 AISEO》《AI搜索来了，John 说别再只围着关键词做 SEO》
  《一夜之间哥飞上新闻，刚好测试 AISEO》《本以为 DeepSeek 天下无敌…》— leave **conclusion-style, confident**
  info across podcasts/events/public writing so AI engines retrieve & cite *you*; your own trafficked
  site is the "样板间" proof for any tool you claim works.
